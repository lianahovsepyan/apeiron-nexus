      TRUST ARCHITECTURE
      Separate computation from proof.
      
        
      
          
      01
      Canonical input
      Exact decimal parsing and a length-prefixed canonical byte form. The SHA-256 of those bytes binds evidence to the exact request.
      
          
      02
      Certificate producer
      Emits candidate witnesses. Its own status is recorded as a producer claim and is never accepted as proof.
      
          
      03
      Semantic validation
      Only whitelisted claim × proof-type pairs are accepted, and each witness is checked against the matrix it claims to describe.
      
          
      04
      Independent verifier
      PASS needs a separate checker whose evidence digest matches. When none exists, the system fails closed to INCOMPLETE.
